As you are not an official representative, I am limited with what I can help you with

What you should do next

Questions about the customer's claim

If the caller can provide basic information about the customer's claim, you can tell them:

  • the progress of the claim
  • how the benefit amount or payment has been calculated
  • how a decision has been reached
  • what is expected to happen next

Making a change or sharing other information

  • Do not report a change of circumstances or share personal information about the customer.
  • Advise the caller to try again with the customer present, so they can answer the security questions.
  • Alternatively, the customer can send identity documents to us by post. They will also need to include the reason for the call today.
